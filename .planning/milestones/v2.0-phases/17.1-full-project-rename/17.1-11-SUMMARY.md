- `src/server/gitignore-capability.ts` is NOT in this plan's `files_modified` list and was not edited, per the mid-wave scope rule.
- Expected to self-resolve once 17.1-12 renames `cumpaIgnoreRule` to the same `RENAME_MAP.repoStateDir`-derived form and `dist/` is rebuilt.
