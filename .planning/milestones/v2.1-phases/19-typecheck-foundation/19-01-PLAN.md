<objective>
Repair the broken `vue-tsc`/TypeScript-7 toolchain using the Microsoft-documented dual npm-alias scheme locked in CONTEXT.md (D-01/D-02), widen `tsconfig.web.json` from its seven-file allowlist to a full-tree `include` glob with the `vite/client` config-gap fix applied in the same edit, and prove the native `build:node` compiler's output is unaffected by the swap.

Purpose: this is the prerequisite infrastructure every other Phase 19 plan depends on — the 19-error/4-file fix-work (19-02, 19-03) cannot even run `vue-tsc` against the widened scope until this toolchain and config land, and the CI-gate plan (19-04) cannot wire `typecheck:web` into `repository-gates` until the script invokes a working compiler.

Output: `vue-tsc@3.3.14` installed and resolving cleanly; `typescript`/`@typescript/native` aliased per CONTEXT.md; `tsconfig.web.json` scoped to the whole `src/web/` tree with the `vite/client` pitfall pre-empted; `typecheck:web`/`typecheck:tests:web` scripts switched to `vue-tsc`; and a committed, repeatable proof that `build:node`'s `dist/` output is byte-identical across the swap (TYPE-02).
</objective>
