<objective>
Register F7/⇧F7/⌥↑/⌥↓ as Monaco `editor.addAction` overrides on all three editor instances (`originalEditor`, `modifiedEditor` in the paired adapter; `editor` in the single-pane adapter), so these shortcuts fire with focus inside Monaco and suppress Monaco's own Diff Review Pane (F7) and move-line (Alt+Up/Down) bindings — per NAV-03 and the hard keyboard-remap constraint ("⌥⇧[ / ⌥⇧] are replaced by ⌥↑/⌥↓ with NO aliases — the old chords must stop working"). Also add the adapter method the inline/side-by-side toggle (NAV-01) needs at runtime.

Purpose: this is the one genuinely new Monaco-API surface in the phase (per RESEARCH.md), fully self-contained in `diff-adapter.ts` + `DiffWorkspace.vue` and independent of the CSS/chrome-component work in other Wave-1 plans — building it standalone keeps the risky Monaco-keybinding-conflict work isolated and testable before `App.vue`'s final wiring (20-07) needs only to supply four callback functions and listen for new events.
Output: adapter-level keybinding overrides + a `renderSideBySide` runtime switch, both exposed as plain Vue props/emits on `DiffWorkspace.vue` ready for `App.vue` to consume in Wave 4.
</objective>
